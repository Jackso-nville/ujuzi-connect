About Ujuzi Connect

Ujuzi means skill in Swahili. Ujuzi Connect exists so that skilled fundis can find jobs, and so that people who need work done can find the right fundi.

For fundis: finding jobs

Skilled fundis across Kenya often depend on referrals and word of mouth, and have no easy way to show what they can do. Ujuzi Connect gives every fundi a profile with their services, skills, prices, photos of past work and reviews. A job board shows open jobs for their services, and clients are shown fundis ranked by how well they match.

Jobs that fit your skills

The job board shows open jobs for the services on your profile, and you can filter by service or area.

Show your work

Add photos of finished jobs and set your own price, per job, per hour or per day.

Rise with good reviews

Fundis are ranked by service, skills, area, availability and reviews, so good work is rewarded.

For clients: finding a fundi

People who need work done often rely on friends, relatives or luck, and find it hard to compare prices or see past work. Ujuzi Connect lets a client describe a job in their own words, then see recommended fundis with their prices, photos and reviews, and choose the one they trust.

Describe it your way

Write the job in your own words. Our assistant suggests the right service, and you can always change it.

Recommendations you can explain

Every recommended fundi shows the reasons for the match, so you know why they were suggested.

Compare before you choose

Prices, photos of past work and reviews are shown up front.

Privacy

Phone numbers stay hidden until a client accepts a fundi's offer, and email addresses are never shown to other users.

What we do not do

  • We do not handle payments. A client and a fundi agree the payment and settle it directly.
  • We do not check qualifications. Fundis write their own profiles, so read reviews and look at photos of past work before you choose.

How Ujuzi Connect works

Whether you need work done or you are a fundi looking for jobs, here is how to get started.

For clients

How can Ujuzi Connect help you?

The possibilities are endless. Ujuzi Connect brings you skilled fundis for repairs, building, painting, plumbing, electrical work and much more. See their prices, photos of past work and reviews, then choose the fundi you trust.

How to hire a fundi

Any size of job

From fixing a socket to building a wardrobe, describe the work and get it done.

Flexible pricing

Fundis set their own price per job, per hour or per day. Compare before you choose, then agree how and when to pay directly with your fundi.

Diverse talent

Plumbers, electricians, carpenters, masons, painters, welders and appliance repairers, all in one place.

How does it work?

  1. Post a job

    Describe what you need in your own words. Our assistant can suggest the right service for you.

  2. Choose a fundi

    We recommend fundis who match your job. Compare their prices, photos of past work and reviews, invite the ones you like, and accept the offer that suits you.

  3. Pay when you are satisfied

    Agree the payment with your fundi and pay them directly once the job is done to your satisfaction. Ujuzi Connect does not handle money.

Keep in contact

Message any fundi who sends you an offer, to ask questions or agree the details. Once you accept an offer, you and your fundi also see each other's phone number. When the job is done, leave a review to help the next client.

For fundis

How do I get started?

Turn your skill into steady work. Set up your profile once, and clients looking for your service can find you.

  1. Complete your profile

    Choose the services you offer, list your skills, and add your area, years of experience and your price. A complete profile ranks better, because the ranking uses your skills, area and availability.

  2. Browse jobs that suit you

    The job board shows open jobs for your services. Filter by service or area, and look out for the "invited you" tag, which means a client picked you.

  3. Write your best description

    Add a short message to each offer, and put photos of your best work on your profile. Say what you will do, and how soon you can start.

  4. Get ranked and earn

    Clients see fundis ranked by service, skills, area, availability and reviews. Do good work, collect good reviews and move up the ranking.

Manage your job

Clients can message you once you send an offer, and you can message them too. When a client accepts your offer, you see their phone number. Agree the day, the details and how you will be paid, do the work, then mark the job completed. The client can then review your work, and the review appears on your profile.

Safe and secure

  • Phone numbers stay private. A client and a fundi see each other's number only after the client accepts an offer. Email addresses are never shown to other users.
  • Passwords are protected. They are stored in scrambled form, never as readable text, and password reset links work only once.
  • Reviews are real. Only the client of a completed job can review the fundi who did it.
  • Check before you choose. Read reviews and look at photos of past work. Ujuzi Connect does not check fundis' qualifications for you, so ask questions before you decide.
  • Agree before work starts. Settle the price and what is included first. Clients pay only when satisfied, and fundis are paid directly. Ujuzi Connect does not handle payments.
  • Only the right fundis see your job. A job is shown to fundis who offer that service.
  • About the assistant. When you use "Suggest a service", the text of your job description is sent to Google's Gemini service to find the right category. Your name and contact details are not sent.
